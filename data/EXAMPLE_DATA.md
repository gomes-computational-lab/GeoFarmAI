# Synthetic example data

`example_field.csv`, `example_soil.csv`, `example_terrain.csv`, and
`example_outcomes.csv` are small deterministic synthetic datasets created for
GeoFarmAI documentation and automated configuration tests. They do not contain
observations from private research datasets and must not be interpreted as
agronomic measurements.

The coordinates use EPSG:32615 and are arranged on a 20 metre demonstration
grid. Values were chosen only to provide two visibly separable synthetic groups
for quick, reproducible smoke tests.
