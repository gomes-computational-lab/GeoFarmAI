from contributions to the broader research. These records do not determine
manuscript authorship. Manuscript authorship must be assessed separately from

## Experimental Provenance

The scientific results used in the research poster or manuscript were
generated with an earlier experimental implementation of the GeoFarmAI
pipeline. Patricia Leigh Cabañes Alegria contributed code to that earlier
implementation. Other student researchers helped run scientific experiments
and generate or analyze the resulting evidence.

GeoFarmAI v0.1.0 is a subsequent refactoring and generalization of that
scientific pipeline. This provenance statement does not claim that v0.1.0
generated the historical poster or manuscript results.
